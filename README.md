## About the project

Officina is a personal research project, a digital space documenting the time I spend reading and learning about design practice, from philosophy and design history to today's challenges. Inside, the reader will find my takes regarding design initiatives, books, exhibitions or talks. I try to keep all my written words AI-free, since it is exactly the time I put (inefficiently) into writing that represents the purpose of this project. I took the name of the repository from my working space ("Officina" means "workshop" or "garage" in Italian) and I was inspired to start design writing by reading Silvio Lorusso: *What Design Can't Do: Essays on Design and Disillusion*, Eindhoven, Set Margins', 2023. <br />
This repository was created in February 2026 and it has been updated periodically ever since.
The texts on the website are written in my mother tongue: Italian.

### Design process

As typeface, Officina uses **Ronzino** by [Collletttivo](https://github.com/collletttivo): Gorlero, L., & Pio Mazzaferro, A. (2023). Workhorse (Version 1.0) [Computer software]. https://github.com/luigigorlero/workhorse

Among other palettes, I choose the **combination number 174** from Sanzo Wada: *A Dictionary of Color Combinations*. Kyoto: Seigensha Art Publishing, 2011, p. 114.
